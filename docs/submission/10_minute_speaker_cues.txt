0:00–2:00  SLIDE 1  Introduction, objectives, contribution
  This project is a local webcam tool for people who cannot speak.
  Camera sees a hand pose → word on screen → Windows speaks it.
  Objectives: landmarks, custom vocabulary, anti-flicker, offline speech, viva-safe labels.
  I built the app, rules, smoothing, speech thread, and tests.
  Honest line: custom vocabulary, not real ASL.

  Walk the five boxes: Input → Landmarks → Classify → Stabilize → Output.
